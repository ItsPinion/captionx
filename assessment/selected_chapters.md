## Why these two

Both chapters carry a healthy mix of legitimate figures: embedded rasters
with printed `Fig.` captions (the caption-matching denominator), shared
captions over multi-raster figures, a portrait with no caption, and
decorative Exercises art — enough variety to exercise the whole pipeline
without a chapter that is nearly figure-free.
